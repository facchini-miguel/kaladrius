# Getting started

## Logging in and out
- Log in with the **username** and **password** the clinic administrator created for you.
- After 5 attempts with a wrong password, access is blocked for 1 hour. If you forgot your password, ask the clinic administrator for a new one.
- To log out, open the menu with your name, at the right of the top bar, and click **Log out**.

## Changing your password
In the menu with your name, click **Change password**. Enter the current password and the new one twice. Do this on your first login if the administrator created the password for you.

## Home page
After logging in, the home page shows the **summary of the day**:
- **Shortcuts:** search for a patient (name, code or CPF), open the schedule, new patient, new estimate and receive.
- **Patients booked today:** how many came, missed and are still to come, and how many already started without attendance marked.
- **No-shows this month:** the share of no-shows among this month's appointments with attendance marked.
- **Today's schedule:** the day's appointments in time order, with the next one highlighted. Mark **✓ Came** or **✗ Missed** right there.
- **Recalls to make:** today's and overdue ones, with the patient's mobile phone. The **✓** button marks the recall as done.
- **Today's birthdays** and **open estimates expiring within 7 days**.

Each person sees only what their role allows:
- **Front desk:** also sees the **daily cash drawer** (open or closed), what was **received today** by payment method and what is **still to receive today**.
- **Administrator:** also sees what was **paid today**, the **result** (received minus paid) of the day and of the month so far, compared with the same days of last month, the **overdue receivables**, the bills **payable within 7 days** and the **balance in the accounts**. Click a box to open the matching screen.
- **Practitioner:** sees only their own schedule, with no amounts of the clinic.

## Language
The system works in **Portuguese, English and Spanish**. Click the **flag** in the top bar, between the clinic name and your name, and choose the language. Everything changes right away, and the choice is saved for your next logins.

## Clinic
If you work in more than one clinic, the current clinic's name shows in the top bar. Click it to switch. Each clinic has its own patients, schedule and team.

## Roles
What you see depends on your role in the clinic:

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| **Front desk** | Every practitioner's schedule, patients (no archiving), recalls and birthdays |
| **Practitioner** | Only their own schedule, patients (no archiving), recalls and birthdays |
| **Clinic administrator** | Everything, including team, practitioners, appointment types, clinic settings, audit log and archived patients |

Buttons you cannot use are not shown.

## What's new
When the system is updated, a notice shows at the top of the screens. In **Help > What's new** you see what changed in each version.
