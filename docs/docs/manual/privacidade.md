# Privacy policy

Version 1 · 10/27/2026

This policy explains how the personal data of patients, guardians, staff and suppliers is processed in **Kaladrius**, the management system the clinic uses: what data, what for, on what legal basis, who has access, where it is stored, for how long and how to exercise your rights. It follows Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13,709/2018) and the law on electronic patient records (Law 13,787/2018).

## 1. Terms used
- **Data subject:** the person the data refers to (for example, the patient).
- **Controller:** whoever decides how and why the data is processed. Here, the clinic.
- **Processor:** whoever processes the data on the controller's behalf, following its instructions.
- **Sensitive personal data:** among others, data concerning health.
- **Processing:** any operation with data, such as collecting, storing, consulting, changing or deleting it.
- **Anonymization:** removing the data that makes it possible to identify the person.

## 2. Who is responsible for your data
The **clinic that treats you** is the controller: it decides how and why the data is used and answers for it to you and to Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (ANPD). Whoever develops and maintains Kaladrius acts as a **processor**, keeping the data on the clinic's behalf and never using it for any other purpose.

**Data subject contact channel (data protection officer):** the clinic's own. In the system, its name, e-mail and phone number appear at the top of this page. Small clinics may keep this contact channel instead of appointing a data protection officer (Resolution CD/ANPD No. 2/2022).

## 3. What data is processed

**Patients**
- Identification: name, CPF or CNPJ (Brazilian tax numbers), RG (ID card), date of birth, sex and occupation.
- Contact: phone numbers, e-mail and address.
- Care: appointments (date, practitioner, type of appointment and notes), recalls and treatment estimates, with the procedures and teeth involved. These are **health data**, which the law treats as sensitive.
- Finance: amounts, installments, payments (date, method and account; for cards, only the brand, number of installments and transaction code, **never the card number**) and receipts.
- Notes written by the team during care.

**Guardians**: for patients who are minors or need a guardian, the guardian's name.

**Clinic team**
- Name, username, e-mail, access profile and language.
- Logins and logouts, with date, time and IP address; history of who created, changed or deleted each record; login attempts with a wrong password (username typed and IP address).
- For practitioners: professional license and commission percentage.

**Suppliers and other people**: names of suppliers and of whoever pays or receives money from the clinic; in bank statements imported for reconciliation, the name the bank shows on each transaction.

The clinic asks only for the data needed for the purposes in section 4. The data is provided by the data subject or the guardian, or written down by the team during care. Keeping it correct helps the clinic treat you well: let it know when something changes.

## 4. What the data is used for and on what legal basis
| Purpose | Legal basis (LGPD) |
|---|---|
| Scheduling appointments, recalling patients, making estimates and following the treatment | Protection of health, in a procedure carried out by health professionals (art. 11, II, f); performance of a contract (art. 7, V) |
| Charging, issuing receipts and keeping financial and accounting records | Performance of a contract (art. 7, V); legal or regulatory obligation (art. 7, II) |
| Keeping the care history for the legal period | Legal or regulatory obligation (art. 7, II, and art. 11, II, a); regular exercise of rights (art. 7, VI, and art. 11, II, d) |
| Giving the team access to the system and paying practitioners | Performance of a contract (art. 7, V) |
| Protecting the system and recording who accessed and changed what | Legitimate interest (art. 7, IX) and duty of security (art. 46) |

The data is **not** used for advertising, **not** sold, **not** subject to automated decisions and **not** shared for economic advantage or with health insurers for risk selection (art. 11, §§ 4 and 5).

**Children and teenagers:** their data is processed in their best interest (art. 14), on the basis of health protection, as the ANPD advises (Statement CD/ANPD No. 1/2023). Parents or guardians may exercise the rights in section 8 on their behalf.

## 5. Who has access
- Only the clinic's team, with personal usernames and passwords, each according to their profile: practitioners see only their own schedule; the front desk does not see payables; finance, commissions and bank statements are for the administrator only. Everyone is bound by **professional secrecy**.
- Each clinic sees only its own data, even when using the same system.
- **Google Cloud**, which provides the server and keeps the backups, as a sub-processor. No other company receives the data through the system: its screens load nothing from third parties.
- Public authorities, when the law or a court order requires.

## 6. Where the data is stored
The server and backups are on **Google Cloud, in the United States** (us-east1 region). This is an international data transfer, made under art. 33 of the LGPD and Resolution CD/ANPD No. 19/2024, with the provider's contractual data protection safeguards. Communication with the system is always encrypted (HTTPS), and the provider encrypts the stored data.

## 7. For how long
- **Records, appointments, estimates and finance:** while there is a relationship with the clinic and then for **at least 20 years from the last entry**, the retention period for patient records (Law 13,787/2018, art. 6), which applies to all health professions, dentistry included, and also covers tax obligations and the clinic's legal defense. After that period, the data may be securely deleted or handed back to the patient.
- Former patients may have their record **archived**: it leaves the team's screens and is visible only to the administrator.
- **Deletion on request**, when the law allows: the record and the estimates are deleted; appointments and financial entries remain, but without identifying the person (they are needed for the schedule and the accounts); the change history also loses the personal data.
- **Login attempts with a wrong password:** 30 days.
- **Backups:** one a day, each deleted after 30 days.

## 8. Your rights
You can ask the clinic, free of charge and at any time (LGPD, art. 18):
- confirmation that your data is processed and access to it;
- correction of incomplete, wrong or outdated data;
- anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary or excessive data, or data processed against the law;
- portability of the data to another service;
- deletion of the data, within the limits of section 7;
- information about whom the data is shared with;
- withdrawal of consent, when consent is the legal basis, and information about the consequences of not consenting;
- objection to processing carried out against the law.

**How to ask:** at the front desk or through the channel in section 2. To protect your data, the clinic may ask you to confirm your identity. Confirmation and access may be given right away, in a simplified form, or within 15 days, in full (art. 19). If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the ANPD, at [gov.br/anpd](https://www.gov.br/anpd).

## 9. Security
- Passwords stored irreversibly (not even the clinic can see them); access blocked for one hour after 5 wrong attempts; sessions end after 8 hours.
- Access by profile and a record of every change.
- Daily backups, with recovery tests.

No system is completely immune to failures. If a security incident occurs that may cause relevant risk or harm, the clinic notifies those affected and the ANPD within 3 business days (art. 48 and Resolution CD/ANPD No. 15/2024).

## 10. Cookies
The system uses only the cookies it needs to work: the session cookie (keeps the person logged in; expires after 8 hours), the form protection cookie and the chosen language (1 year). There are no advertising, analytics or third-party cookies.

## 11. Changes to this policy
When this policy changes, the new version is published on this page, with its date. Important changes are also announced at the clinic.

## 12. Applicable law
This policy follows Brazilian law. Questions or disputes may be taken to the ANPD, to consumer protection agencies or to the courts of the data subject's place of residence.
