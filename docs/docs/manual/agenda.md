# Schedule

## Viewing the schedule
- In **Schedule**, choose the practitioner in the list next to the title. The system remembers the last choice and, on the same day, the week you were looking at: leaving the schedule and coming back shows that week again (the next day it opens on today).
- Use the arrows to go to the previous or next week, **today** to come back, or the date field to jump to a day.
- Colors show the **appointment type** (the legend is above the grid).
- The header of each day shows the **birthdays** (🎂) and the **pending recalls** (📞). Click to see the list.
- On mobile phones, the schedule opens in the day view.

**Practitioners** see only their own schedule. If the schedule is empty with a notice, ask the administrator to link your user to your practitioner record.

## Booking
1. Click a free slot, or drag over several slots.
2. Choose the **appointment type** and the **patient** (type part of the name, the code or the CPF and pick from the list).
3. Without a registered patient, write a **description** (for example, "Meeting" or "Blocked").
4. To repeat every week, fill in **Repeat for how many weeks** (1 = only on this date).
5. Click **Save**.

Times follow the clinic's slot length (for example, every 30 minutes) and must be within the clinic's opening hours.

## Conflicts
The system does not allow two appointments at the same time for the same practitioner. The screen shows who the conflict is with. In a series, if one of the dates conflicts, nothing is saved.

## Attendance
Open the appointment to mark the patient's **status** (you can also mark it on the home page):
- **✓ Came** and **✗ Missed:** from the day of the appointment on.
- **Cancelled:** at any time. The appointment stays on the schedule, faded and struck through, for the history and the no-show figures, but the **slot becomes free** for another patient.
- **Undo mark:** back to "booked". A cancelled appointment only comes back if nobody took the slot.

On the grid, ✓ marks who came and ✗ who missed. If the appointment is moved to a future day, or left without a patient, the mark is undone.

## Changing and deleting
Click the appointment to open it. Change it and save, or click **Delete**. If it is part of a series, choose to delete **only this one** or **this one and the next ones**. Only administrators and the front desk can delete.
