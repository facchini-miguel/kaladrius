# Patients

## Searching
In **Patients > Patient list**, type part of the **name** (accents not needed), the **code** or the beginning of the **CPF/CNPJ**. The filters choose the type (individual or company) and the status.

## Adding and changing
Click **New patient**. Choose **individual** or **company**: the fields change with the type.
- CPF, CNPJ, phones and postal code (CEP) can be typed with or without punctuation.
- The system checks whether the CPF or CNPJ is valid and whether it already exists in the clinic.
- Choose the **state** and then the **city**. Once the city is chosen, **Neighborhood** and **Street** suggest its known names (the neighborhoods and streets that came from the old system and the ones the clinic already typed for other patients); any other can still be typed.
- Phones need the area code (DDD), and the system checks that it exists. A mobile typed the old way, with 8 digits (such as 54 8123-4567), gets the leading 9 by itself; without the area code and the 9, the WhatsApp link does not open.
- Fill in **WhatsApp** (it may be the same number as the cell phone): the patient record and the recalls on the home page get a link that opens the chat. The old system's fax and MSN are not asked for anymore.

## Patient record
Click the name to open the record: details, contact, address, notes, **upcoming and latest appointments** (click the date to go to the week in the schedule) and **recalls**.

**Print sheet** opens the registration sheet ready to print or save as PDF: details, contact, address, health plan cards, related people, notes and a signature line.

## Related people
In **Related people** on the record, **New related person** adds a relative or contact: the relationship (mother, father, legal guardian, spouse, child, emergency contact…) and either **another patient of the clinic**, found by name, or the **name and phone** of someone who is not a patient. A link to another patient shows on both records ("Mother of Ana" on the mother's), and that patient's own phone is shown. The link is only registration information: it gives no access to the other person's data.

## Active, inactive and archived

| Status | What for | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| **Active** | Patient in treatment | The whole team |
| **Inactive** | Stopped coming, but is still a patient. Uncheck "active" in the record | The whole team, in the "Inactive" filter. Not shown in birthdays |
| **Archived** | Out of daily use: duplicate record, created by mistake, someone who was never a patient | Only the administrator, in the "Archived" filter |

The **administrator** archives with the **Archive** button of the record and can **restore** later. Nothing is lost: appointments, recalls and history are kept. An archived patient does not show in search, the schedule, birthdays or recalls. A patient with open entries in Finance (to receive or to pay) can only be archived after each one is received or cancelled; the **Archive** page lists them, with a link to each.

## Permanent deletion
Only for when the **patient asks for their data to be erased** (LGPD, the Brazilian data protection law). The administrator archives the patient and, in their record, clicks **Delete permanently**. The record and recalls are deleted, appointments stay in the schedule without the patient and financial entries stay without their name; the notes of those appointments, entries and payments are erased too. First, check whether the clinic must keep the clinical records (the law requires 20 years). It cannot be undone.
