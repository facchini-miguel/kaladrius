# Estimates

In **Patients > Estimates**, practitioners, the front desk and the administrator write treatment estimates. You can also start from the patient record, in the **Estimates** panel.

## Writing an estimate
1. Click **New estimate**, choose the patient and the practitioner, the date and the validity (30 days by default). For practitioners, the estimate already comes in their name.
2. In **Add procedure**, click the **teeth** on the dental chart (the quantity follows the number of teeth), choose the **procedure** from the price list and, if you want, the **surfaces or region** (for example, MOD or "upper arch"). Without description or price, those of the price list are used; for an item not in the list, type the description and the price.
3. Repeat for each procedure. To take an item out, click **remove**.

The **dental chart** uses FDI numbering: upper teeth on top, lower teeth below and baby teeth in the middle rows, with the patient's right on the left of the screen. An estimate without teeth applies to the whole mouth.

Only the administrator can give a **discount** on the total.

## Printing
**Print** opens the estimate ready to print or save as PDF, with the clinic's details, the dental chart with the marked teeth, the items, the total, the validity and signature lines.

## Approving or refusing
- **Approve** (front desk and administrator): choose the number of installments, the 1st due date and the category. The estimate becomes a **receivable** and, if the patient paid the 1st installment on the spot, the payment is recorded right away. Once approved, the estimate no longer changes.
- **Refuse**: marks the estimate as refused.

Open estimates past their validity show as **Overdue**.

## Procedure price list
In **Records > Procedure price list**, the administrator registers each procedure with its price and income category. Changing a price does not change estimates already made. To stop using a procedure, uncheck **active**.
