# Medical record

The medical record keeps the **anamnesis of each visit**. Only **practitioners** see it and write in it: the front desk never does, and an administrator only when also registered as a practitioner (**Records > Practitioners**, linked to their user).

## Recording a visit
- From the **schedule**: open the patient's appointment and click **Record visit** (or **Open visit**, when it already exists). The visit is linked to the appointment and takes its date.
- From the **patient record**: in the **Medical record** card, click **New visit**, for a visit outside the schedule.

The anamnesis has five fields, all optional: **chief complaint**, **history of present illness**, **history** (personal and family, current medications, allergies), **physical examination** and **management and plan**.

## Draft, finalize and addenda
- **Save draft** keeps the visit open: only its author can change it.
- **Save and finalize** closes it for good: from then on, nobody changes it. Corrections and later notes are **addenda**, each with its date and practitioner; any practitioner of the clinic can add one.
- The **Medical record** card of the patient record lists the visits, newest first, marked as draft or finalized.

## Printing, certificates and prescriptions
- **Print visit** (on a finalized visit) opens it ready to print or save as PDF, with the clinic's header, the anamnesis, the addenda and a signature line. Without a digital signature, the printed and signed copy is the legal record.
- In the **Certificates and prescriptions** card, the visit's author issues:
  - a **certificate** of **attendance** (date and times, filled in from the appointment) or of **leave** (number of days), with optional notes;
  - a simple **prescription**, one item per line (medicine, dose and how to use it). Controlled drugs need the official forms, which the system does not issue.
- An issued document never changes; it is kept in the medical record and can be printed again by any practitioner.

## Privacy and retention
- The **audit log** records who created, changed, finalized or **opened** each visit, but never copies the clinical text.
- The medical record is kept for **20 years** (Law 13.787/2018): archiving the patient keeps it, and a patient with a medical record cannot be permanently deleted.
- ICD codes are not available yet.
