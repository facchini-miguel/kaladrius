# Recalls and birthdays

## Recalls
A recall is a reminder for a patient's follow-up: "6-month check-up", "remove stitches" and so on.

- In **Recalls**, the list shows the **pending** ones, with the **overdue** ones in red and **today's** in yellow. Filters show the done ones or all of them, by period and by name.
- Click **New recall**, choose the patient, the date and the description. You can also create one from the patient record.
- After talking to the patient, click **Mark as done**. If needed, **Reopen** makes the recall pending again.
- Pending recalls show in the schedule, in the header of each day (📞).

## Birthdays
In **Patients > Birthdays**, choose the month to see the active patients with birthdays, with the age they will turn, phone and e-mail. Today's are highlighted. In the schedule, each day's birthdays show in the header (🎂).
