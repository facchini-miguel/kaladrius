# Team and practitioners

These screens belong to the **clinic administrator**, in the **Records** menu.

## Team
In **Records > Team**, everyone who uses the system in the clinic.
- **New member:** username, name, e-mail, role (front desk, practitioner or administrator) and a first password. Give the password to the person and ask them to change it on first login.
- **Edit:** changes the role and the access. To remove someone from the team, uncheck **Access to this clinic allowed**: the history of who did what is kept.
- **Set new password:** for those who forgot their password.
- Nobody changes their own role, and the clinic must always have an active administrator.
- If the person also works in another clinic, name, e-mail and password can only be changed by them or by support.

## Practitioners
In **Records > Practitioners**, those who see patients: name, professional license (CRO, CRM…), schedule color and **login user**.

Link each practitioner to their user in the **login user** field. Without that link, the practitioner logs in but sees no schedule.

To hide a practitioner who left without losing the history, uncheck **active**.
