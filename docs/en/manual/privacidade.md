# Privacy policy

Version 1 · 10/27/2026

This policy explains which personal data the clinic keeps in Medical System, what for, who has access, where it is stored, for how long and how you exercise your rights, under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13,709/2018).

## 1. Who is responsible for your data
The **clinic that treats you** is the data controller: it decides how and why the data is used. Medical System is the system the clinic uses for that; whoever develops and maintains it acts as a **processor**, keeping the data on the clinic's behalf and never using it for any other purpose.

**Privacy contact (data protection officer):** the clinic itself. In the system, its name, e-mail and phone number appear at the top of this page.

## 2. What data is kept

**Patients**
- Identification: name, CPF or CNPJ (Brazilian tax numbers), RG (ID card), date of birth, sex, occupation and, for minors, the name of the guardian.
- Contact: phone numbers, e-mail and address.
- Care: appointments (date, practitioner, type of appointment and notes), recalls and treatment estimates, with the procedures and teeth involved. These are **health data**, which the law treats as sensitive.
- Finance: amounts, installments, payments (date, method and account; for cards, only the brand, number of installments and transaction code, **never the card number**) and receipts. The clinic's bank statements, imported for reconciliation, may show the name of whoever made a payment.
- Notes written by the team.

**Clinic team**
- Name, username, e-mail, access profile and language.
- Logins and logouts, with date, time and IP address; history of who created, changed or deleted each record; login attempts with a wrong password (username typed and IP address).
- For practitioners: professional license and commission percentage.

## 3. What the data is used for
| Purpose | Legal basis (LGPD) |
|---|---|
| Scheduling appointments, recalling patients, making estimates and following the treatment | Protection of health (art. 11, II, f) and performance of a contract (art. 7, V) |
| Charging, issuing receipts and keeping financial and accounting records | Performance of a contract (art. 7, V) and legal obligation (art. 7, II) |
| Protecting the system and recording who accessed and changed what | Legitimate interest and duty of security (art. 7, IX, and art. 46) |

The data is **not** used for advertising, **not** sold and **not** subject to automated decisions.

## 4. Who has access
- Only the clinic's team, with personal usernames and passwords, each according to their profile: practitioners see only their own schedule; the front desk does not see payables; finance, commissions and bank statements are for the administrator only.
- Each clinic sees only its own data, even when using the same system.
- **Google Cloud**, where the server and backups are. No other company receives the data: the system's screens load nothing from third parties.
- Public authorities, when the law or a court order requires.

## 5. Where the data is stored
The server and backups are on **Google Cloud, in the United States** (us-east1 region). This is an international data transfer (LGPD, art. 33), made under the provider's contractual data protection safeguards. Communication with the system is always encrypted (HTTPS), and the provider encrypts the stored data.

## 6. For how long
- **Records, appointments, estimates and finance:** while there is a relationship with the clinic and then for **20 years** from the last entry, the retention period for patient records under Law 13,787/2018 (which applies to all health professions, dentistry included). This period also covers tax obligations and the clinic's legal defense.
- Former patients may have their record **archived**: it leaves the team's screens and is visible only to the administrator.
- **Deletion on request**, when the law allows: the record and the estimates are deleted; appointments and financial entries remain, but without identifying the person (they are needed for the schedule and the accounts); the change history also loses the personal data.
- **Login attempts with a wrong password:** 30 days.
- **Backups:** one a day, each deleted after 30 days.

## 7. Your rights
You can ask the clinic, at any time (LGPD, art. 18):
- confirmation that your data is processed and a copy of it;
- correction of incomplete, wrong or outdated data;
- anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data or data processed against the law;
- portability of the data to another service;
- information about whom the data is shared with;
- deletion of the data, within the limits of section 6.

Make the request at the front desk or through the contact in section 1. The clinic answers within 15 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (ANPD), at gov.br/anpd.

## 8. Security
- Passwords stored irreversibly (not even the clinic can see them); access blocked for one hour after 5 wrong attempts; sessions end after 8 hours.
- Access by profile and a record of every change.
- Daily backups, with recovery tests.

In case of a security incident that may cause relevant risk or harm, the clinic notifies those affected and the ANPD (LGPD, art. 48).

## 9. Cookies
The system uses only the cookies it needs to work: the session cookie (keeps the person logged in; expires after 8 hours), the form protection cookie and the chosen language (1 year). There are no advertising, analytics or third-party cookies.

## 10. Changes to this policy
When this policy changes, the new version is published on this page, with its date.
