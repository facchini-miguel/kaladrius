# Brand and press

Logo files, colors and short texts for anyone writing about Kaladrius: press, blogs, presentations and partners.

## Logo

Every file is available as **SVG** (vector, any size) and **PNG** (transparent background: 1024 px for the symbol and the icon, 256 px tall for the logo). Prefer the SVG whenever the tool accepts it.

<KitDeMarca />

- **Logo:** the symbol with the name. Use it whenever there is room.
- **Symbol:** the bird alone, for small spaces and avatars.
- **App icon:** the white symbol on a blue square with rounded corners, for app stores, social network avatars and shortcuts.
- **White** versions go on dark or colored backgrounds; **black** versions are for printing in one color.

## The symbol

The symbol is the head of the **caladrius**, the white bird of medieval bestiaries that cures the sick by looking at them. The eye, cut out of the drawing, stands for that look of care; the circle around it is the patient's whole history, kept in one place.

## How to use

- Keep free space around the logo of at least the width of the ring's line times three.
- Do not use the symbol smaller than **16 px**, or the logo narrower than **96 px**.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolor, outline or add shadows to the logo, and do not rebuild it with another font.
- Do not place the blue logo on busy photos or on backgrounds with little contrast: use the white version.
- Write the name as **Kaladrius**, with a capital K, in any language. Not "KalaDrius", "Caladrius" or "KALADRIUS" in running text.

The Kaladrius name and logo are not covered by the Apache License 2.0 of the code (its section 6 does not grant trademark rights). You may use them to refer to the project, in articles, talks and integrations, without changing them and without suggesting that the project endorses you or your product. A modified version of the software (a fork) must use another name and logo.

## About Kaladrius

**One sentence:**

> Kaladrius is open-source software for clinics: schedule, patients, estimates, recalls and finance in one web app, which any clinic can install on its own server.

**One paragraph:**

> Kaladrius is open-source management software for medical and dental clinics. It brings together the schedule, patient records, treatment estimates, recalls, finance and the team in a web app that runs in any browser and serves several clinics in one installation. It is free under the Apache License 2.0 and can be installed with Docker on the clinic's own server or on the Google Cloud free tier. The name comes from the caladrius, the white bird of medieval legends that cures the sick by looking at them.
